## Notation
- $(x_n)$: a sequence. $x_n \to L$: it converges to $L$. AC: absolutely convergent.
- $(\exists N)(\forall n \geq N)$: "eventually", i.e. on a tail.
- $(\forall N)(\exists n \geq N)$: "for infinitely many $n$".
- $\sum x_n$: the series (convergent or not). $\sum_1^\infty x_n$: its sum, when it converges.
- $O$, $o$, $\sim$: see [[A1 - Sequences and Convergence#Complex Sequences|asymptotic notation]].

## Key equivalents of completeness
Each of these (with the Archimedean property where noted) is equivalent to the completeness axiom:
- bounded monotone sequences converge;
- Nested Intervals Theorem (+ Archimedean);
- Bolzano–Weierstrass;
- Cauchy sequences converge (+ Archimedean).
